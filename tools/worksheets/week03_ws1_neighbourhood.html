C Adana or Saimbeyli? Write sentences. /6

🏙️ Adana
  • a big city · about 2 million people
  • very hot in summer
  • crowded and noisy · on low, flat land
🏔️ Saimbeyli
  • a small town · about 15 thousand people
  • cool in summer, cold and snowy in winter
  • quiet and green · high in the Taurus Mountains

Example: Adana / big / Saimbeyli → Adana is bigger than Saimbeyli.  ·  small → smaller · big → bigger · noisy → noisier · crowded → more crowded
